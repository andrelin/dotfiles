## Round 13

### R13-2 · major · plan conformance
Spec 4 says "a failed invoice never blocks the rest of the batch", but `syncInvoices()` stops at the first invoice whose
ledger call returns 409 Conflict, leaving the rest unsent until the next nightly run.

## Round 14

### R14-6 · major · correctness
Spec 7 says "the ledger and our invoices table never disagree at the end of a run". Continuing past a 409 (as R13-2 asks)
leaves that invoice marked `sent` locally while the ledger rejected it, so the two disagree until someone reconciles by hand.
